Marvel Ad Campaign

A multi-platform promotional campaign designed to engage audiences across digital, print, and interactive media — crafted to highlight iconic storytelling at scale.

Creative Direction Production Lead Digital Marketing

Bringing Heroes to Every Screen.

The Marvel Ad Campaign was built to create unified narrative cohesion across diverse touchpoints. The objective was clear: transform high-energy artwork and video assets into an immersive narrative experience.

From initial concept sketches through final digital distribution, every asset was engineered for maximum visual impact, optimized for both desktop and mobile platforms.

Campaign Deliverables

  • High-Resolution Digital Key Art
  • Interactive Social Ad Placement
  • Motion Graphics & Video Teasers
  • Localized Marketing Guidelines

Campaign Pillars & Assets

Four core beats designed to drive audience engagement throughout the rollout.

01. Teaser Art & Teasers

Initial teaser imagery released across social channels to generate early momentum and conversation.

02. Character Spotlights

Dedicated digital cards showcasing specific heroes, custom-tailored for mobile feeds.

03. Interactive Ads

Dynamic rich-media web banners allowing users to explore story previews directly in-browser.

04. Key Art & Print

Full-scale promotional posters for high-visibility physical and digital outdoor placements.
